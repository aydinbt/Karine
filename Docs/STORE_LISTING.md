## Full description

Night. A desk lamp. A new file on your desk.

Karine is a player-driven detective game. Nobody tells you where to look next, and nothing on screen marks who is lying. You read, you compare, you decide.

OPEN THE FILE
Police reports, message logs, camera frames, witness statements and site plans arrive on your desk. A statement can make a new record available — but you choose what to open.

COMPARE
Line up times, names and places. Spot what doesn't fit. The game never flags a contradiction for you.

QUESTION
Call people in and confront them with records — but only the ones that mention them. Ask the questions your reading has opened up.

REPORT
Name the person, the method and the evidence. A lucky guess is not a supported report, and your superiors will notice the difference.

A CAREER, NOT A LEVEL LIST
Start in Istanbul and work your way through ten countries — the UK, Germany, Japan, France, the USA, Italy, Spain, Canada and Australia. Each chapter adds a new investigative tool and keeps the old ones. Threads from one country surface in the next.

Your trust score rises with well-reasoned reports and falls with careless ones. Lose it and you are put on supervised desk duty until you earn it back.

FEATURES
• 73 hand-crafted, interlinked cases
• No hidden markers, no automatic "lie" labels — every conclusion is yours
• Pixel-art noir desk with an optional CRT look
• Text size and high-contrast options
• English, Turkish, German and French
• Cloud save: your career follows you to a new phone
• Free to play; remove ads with a one-time purchase

Karine is a work of fiction. Its people, institutions and events are imaginary.
