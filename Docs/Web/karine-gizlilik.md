- **Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.:** Firebase (bulut kaydı, kimlik, çökme raporu) ve AdMob (reklam)

Bu hizmetlerin sunucuları Türkiye dışında, AB'de ve ABD'de bulunabilir. Yurt dışına aktarım KVKK m.9'a uygun güvencelerle yapılır: [STANDART SÖZLEŞME / DİĞER — HUKUKÇU DOLDURACAK].

## Hakların ve silme

KVKK m.11 kapsamında, AB'deysen GDPR kapsamında şu hakların vardır:

- verilerine erişmek
- verilerini düzeltmek
- verilerini sildirmek
- işlemeye itiraz etmek
- şikâyette bulunmak

**Oyunun içinden silme:** Ayarlar › Hesap › "Hesabı ve verileri sil". Buluttaki kaydın, oyuncu kimliğin ve telefondaki ilerlemen hemen ve kalıcı olarak silinir.

**Oyuna erişemiyorsan:** [hesap silme sayfasındaki](https://bubegames.com/karine/hesap-silme) adımları izle ya da [E-POSTA] adresine yaz. Talebini en geç 30 gün içinde sonuçlandırırız.

Kişiselleştirilmiş reklam iznini, cihaz ayarlarından ya da oyundaki izin formundan istediğin zaman geri alabilirsin.

## Çocuklar

Karine suç soruşturması temalı bir oyundur ve 13 yaşından küçük çocuklara yönelik değildir. 13 yaşından küçük bir çocuğa ait veri işlediğimizi fark edersen bize yaz; sileriz.

## In short

Karine never asks for your name, email, phone number or location. To protect your progress, we keep only a random player ID and your career save. You can delete all of it from inside the game at any time.

## What we process

| Data | Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Random player ID (not linked to your identity) | Keeping your save yours | Performance of contract |
| Career save: closed files, rank, trust score, unlocked rewards, play time | Restoring your progress after you reinstall or change phones | Performance of contract |
| If you sign in with Apple or Google, the account ID that service provides | Moving your save to another device | Performance of contract |
| IP address and technical logs while connected (Google Firebase) | Running the service securely | Legitimate interest |
| Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): device model, OS version, error log | Finding and fixing bugs | Legitimate interest |
| Advertising ID and ad interactions (Google AdMob) | Showing ads; personalising them only if you consent | Consent for personalisation; legitimate interest otherwise |
| Purchase receipt (Apple / Google store) | Recognising and restoring "Remove ads" and "Permanent priority" | Performance of contract |

What you do inside a case (what you read, whom you questioned, your notes) stays on your phone and is never sent to our servers.

We never see your payment details; the store handles payment.

So that your player ID survives a reinstall, the game keeps a short key in the device's secure storage (iOS Keychain, Android Google Block Store).

## Who receives it

We do not sell data. It is shared only with the processors that run the service:

- **Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.:** Firebase (cloud save, sign-in, crash reports) and AdMob (ads)
- **Apple Inc. and Google LLC:** app stores, sign-in and purchases

Their servers may be located outside your country, including in the EU and the US.

## Retention

Your career save and player ID are kept until you delete them.

## Your rights and deletion

**In the game:** Settings › Account › "Delete account and data". Your cloud save, player ID and on-device progress are deleted immediately and permanently.

**If you cannot open the game:** follow the steps on the [account deletion page](https://bubegames.com/karine/hesap-silme) or email [EMAIL]. We complete requests within 30 days.

You may withdraw consent for personalised ads at any time, in your device settings or in the in-game consent form.

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, Turkey's KVKK or US state laws), you may also have the right to access, correct, port or object to the processing of your data, and to complain to your data protection authority.

## Children

Karine is a crime-investigation game and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe we hold data from a child under 13, contact us and we will delete it.

If we update this policy, the date above changes. Significant changes are also announced in the game.
